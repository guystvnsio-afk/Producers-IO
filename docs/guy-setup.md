# Guy setup

Agents do not create accounts, register domains, or enter card numbers. This is the list only you can do. Do it before any Astra job is pasted.

## 1. Register the domain

`producersio.com` still returned HTTP 404 from Verisign RDAP on 1 Oct 2026 (`https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/producersio.com`). Register it in your name. A 404 is not a trademark clearance.

## 2. Search the trademark before any ad spend

Search Producers IO in the USPTO trademark search: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search

Also look at similar marks in insurance, CRM, and marketing services. One unrelated name already in the wild is PRODUCER by CREE8, a media tool at `the-producer.io`. That is not a clearance and not a conflict ruling. No ads run until you write down the search date and whether you are going ahead.

## 3. Start HighLevel

Use the public pricing page only: https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing

- Choose the Pro card. The page lists it at $4,970 per year and the FAQ still describes SaaS Mode as the $497/mo Pro plan. In checkout, confirm you are starting Agency Pro with SaaS Mode, monthly at $497, unless you deliberately want the annual charge.
- The button on that card is a 14-day trial. Do not use an affiliate link that offers 30 days.
- The trial bills on its own when it ends. Usage charges for phone and email can start during the trial.
- The day you start, put a reminder on day 12. If you start on 1 Oct 2026, day 12 is 13 Oct 2026 and the trial ends 15 Oct 2026.

## 4. Create the master sub-account

Create one sub-account named `Producers IO Master`. Build nothing in a live client account. Tell this repo when that sub-account exists. Until then every file in `astra-jobs/` stays unpasted.

## 5. Turn on Workflow AI

In the agency account open Agency Settings, then Labs, and enable Workflow AI Builder.

On 1 Oct 2026 the HighLevel help center still said Workflow AI Builder is free with a daily fair-use limit, and that it is not billed as AI credits: https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product-pricing

The Summer of AI window that made several AI tools free ended 31 Aug 2026. On day 1, confirm inside your account whether a build still costs credits. Write the answer in `docs/open-items.md`.

## 6. Payments

Connect Stripe yourself, in test mode first. Agents never see the card, the Stripe secret, or the HighLevel password.
