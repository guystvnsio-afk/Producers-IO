# Care package copy outline

This is the physical kit a member’s customer receives. It is not mailed to the member. It is a reminder from the agent, not a policy and not advice about coverage.

## Page 1 — welcome

- Agent’s name and business, from custom values.
- One sentence: the household can keep this with the papers they already have.
- The agent’s phone, printed from the location number, not typed into the template as a personal cell.
- No carrier. No premium. No Medicare. No claim that a policy is in force.

## Page 2 — family sheet

- Who to contact first: the agent.
- Where this magnet should go: the fridge.
- What to gather before calling: the papers the household already keeps, and the names of the people in the house.
- One referral line: if someone they know wants the same kind of help, they can text the agent.
- No product pitch and no rate.

## MagnaCard

Front: agent name, business name, phone, and “Keep this on the fridge.”

Back: blank except a short line, “Questions go to the agent on the front.” No carrier logo.

## Invoice line the member sees

`Care package for {customer first name} — 2 × mail cost`

Do not describe it as a gift shop item. Do not add a handling fee on top of the 2×.
