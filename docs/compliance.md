# Compliance checklist

Run this on every ad, page, text, email, letter, and magnet. A miss on one line sends the piece back. Do not soften the line to ship.

- No age, birth year, or “seniors” call-out in anything an agent will run as an ad, or in our own ads.
- No carrier name.
- No premium, rate, or “low cost.”
- Nothing about Medicare.
- No guaranteed result, no income claim, and no “you will save this much.”
- No testimonial, including a made-up one.
- No founder face and no stock photo presented as a customer.
- Every text sequence stops on STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, QUIT, or END.
- Messages wait for 08:00–20:00 in the contact’s time zone.
- Care-package copy is about the agent and the household, not about a product from a carrier.
- The client line is not named as iMessage in anything a customer reads.
- Lead workflows W01–W04 do not use the client line.
- Names, numbers, and links are custom values.

Our own ads sell to agents and still follow this list.
